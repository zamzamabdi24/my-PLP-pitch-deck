# MavunoLink Pitch Deck

## Project Overview

MavunoLink is a mobile-first marketplace that connects smallholder farmers directly with reliable food buyers.

The platform helps farmers access better markets and helps restaurants, schools, retailers, processors, and other institutions source produce more consistently.

## Problem

Smallholder farmers often depend on brokers and informal markets to sell their produce. They may not know current market prices, have limited access to buyers, and experience losses when produce cannot be sold quickly.

At the same time, food buyers spend significant time searching for reliable suppliers and may receive inconsistent quantities, quality, and delivery times.

The main problem is a lack of efficient, trusted, and transparent coordination between farmers and buyers.

## Solution

MavunoLink provides a simple marketplace where:

1. Farmers list available produce.
2. Buyers post orders and requirements.
3. The platform matches supply with demand.
4. Delivery is coordinated.
5. Payments and transaction records are tracked digitally.

The service can begin through a lightweight web platform supported by WhatsApp and SMS to make it accessible to users with limited internet access or digital experience.

## Target Customers

### Primary customers

- Smallholder farmers.
- Farmer cooperatives.
- Farmer groups.

### Secondary customers

- Restaurants and hotels.
- Schools and hospitals.
- Retail shops and supermarkets.
- Food processors.
- Wholesalers.

The initial pilot will focus on vegetable farmers and institutional buyers in one urban market.

## Product Features

- Farmer registration.
- Buyer registration.
- Produce listings.
- Buyer order requests.
- Transparent pricing.
- Supply and demand matching.
- Delivery coordination.
- Digital payment records.
- Ratings and trust profiles.
- SMS and WhatsApp notifications.
- Sales and income reports.

## Value Proposition

### For farmers

- More buyer access.
- Better price information.
- Reduced dependence on brokers.
- More predictable demand.
- Better payment tracking.
- Reduced produce waste.

### For buyers

- Easier supplier discovery.
- More reliable produce supply.
- Consistent ordering.
- Better quality and quantity visibility.
- Reduced sourcing time.
- Improved delivery coordination.

## Business Model

MavunoLink will earn revenue through:

- A commission on completed transactions.
- Delivery coordination fees.
- Premium subscriptions for large buyers.
- Data and market insights for agribusiness partners.
- Referral fees from financial service providers.

The platform will use transparent pricing and avoid hidden fees.

## Go-to-Market Plan

### Pilot phase

- Recruit 50–100 farmers through cooperatives.
- Register 10–20 buyers.
- Focus on one crop category.
- Coordinate early transactions manually.
- Measure transaction success and repeat usage.

### Growth phase

- Add more farmer groups and buyers.
- Introduce recurring orders.
- Partner with delivery providers.
- Use customer referrals.
- Expand to more crops and locations.

### Partnerships

Potential partners include:

- Farmer cooperatives.
- Agricultural extension organizations.
- NGOs.
- Logistics providers.
- Digital payment providers.
- Financial institutions.
- Food service companies.

## Competitive Advantage

MavunoLink will compete with brokers, open-air markets, WhatsApp groups, and traditional wholesalers.

Its advantage will come from combining:

- Verified users.
- Transparent pricing.
- Digital order records.
- Buyer and farmer ratings.
- Demand and supply data.
- Delivery coordination.
- Local agricultural relationships.
- Low-bandwidth access through SMS and WhatsApp.

## Six-Month Goals

- Interview 50 farmers and 20 buyers.
- Recruit 50 pilot farmers.
- Register 10 recurring buyers.
- Complete 100 transactions.
- Measure farmer income improvement.
- Measure reduction in produce waste.
- Measure buyer satisfaction.
- Validate repeat orders.
- Launch a functional MVP.

## Current Stage

MavunoLink is at the idea and validation stage.

The immediate priority is to conduct customer interviews, validate the most important crop and market segment, and test the first transactions manually before investing heavily in software development.
