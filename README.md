# QUESTION 1

## Using the PLP template, build the first draft of your own pitch deck covering all 12 slides. Focus on getting the content right before polishing the design.

## My Answer

I will build a first-draft pitch deck using the PLP template. The deck will contain all 12 required slides and will focus on clear content before design and polishing.

## Pitch Deck Slides

### Slide 1: Cover
This slide will include:
- Business name
- One-line business description
- Founder name
- Contact information

### Slide 2: Problem
This slide will explain:
- The target customers
- The specific problem they face
- Why the problem is important
- The current solutions and their limitations

### Slide 3: Solution
This slide will explain:
- The product or service being offered
- How it solves the customer problem
- The main benefit to customers
- What makes the solution different

### Slide 4: Market Opportunity
This slide will explain:
- The target market
- The business location
- The size of the market
- Trends that support the business idea

### Slide 5: Product
This slide will explain:
- The main features of the product
- How customers will use it
- How the product works step by step
- Screenshots, mockups, or diagrams if available

### Slide 6: Business Model
This slide will explain:
- How the business will make money
- The pricing model
- Main business costs
- Expected profit margins

### Slide 7: Go-to-Market
This slide will explain:
- How the business will reach customers
- Marketing channels to be used
- Partnerships that will help customer acquisition
- The first target location or customer group

### Slide 8: Competition
This slide will explain:
- Existing competitors or alternatives
- Strengths and weaknesses of competitors
- The business competitive advantage
- Why customers will choose this solution

### Slide 9: Traction
This slide will explain:
- Current users, customers, or signups
- Revenue or pilot results
- Customer feedback
- Partnerships or product progress
- If there is no traction yet, customer interviews, surveys, or prototype testing will be included

### Slide 10: Team
This slide will include:
- Team member names
- Roles and responsibilities
- Relevant skills
- Previous experience

### Slide 11: Financials
This slide will explain:
- Expected revenue
- Major expenses
- Projected growth
- Expected break-even point

### Slide 12: The Ask
This slide will explain:
- The support needed
- Funding required, if any
- Mentorship or partnerships needed
- How the support will help achieve the next milestones

## Focus
The first draft will prioritize:
- Clear problem and solution
- Simple and understandable content
- Realistic business details
- Short bullet points
- Evidence of customer need or traction
- A clear final ask
